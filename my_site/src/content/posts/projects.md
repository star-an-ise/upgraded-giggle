## What I built

The project brings together:

- Mathematical formulation of the problem
- Algorithms and data structures
- Computational experiments
- Visualisations and results
- An implementation built from the ideas above

## What I learned

The most useful part of a project is often not the final result.

Working through the problem made me think more carefully about how mathematical intuition translates into algorithms, where abstractions help, and where they get in the way.

It also produced the usual collection of bugs, failed approaches, and solutions that only became obvious after staring at the problem for far too long.
